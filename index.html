From ceremony to café

Buna is more than a drink in Ethiopia — it is how neighbours meet, deals are made and guests are honoured. Buna House keeps the ceremony at the centre of the room: green beans roasted over charcoal, ground by hand, brewed in a clay jebena and poured in three rounds — Abol, Tona and Bereka. Alongside it, our bar serves modern espresso drinks made from the same day's roast.

Roasted daily

Yirgacheffe, Sidamo and Guji lots, roasted in small batches each morning and rested no more than ten days before brewing.

Ceremony hours

Full traditional ceremonies at 10:00 and 16:00 daily — come sit, the third round is on us for first-time guests.

Fair to farmers

We buy through two washing stations we have worked with for years and publish the farm-gate price on the board by the counter.
